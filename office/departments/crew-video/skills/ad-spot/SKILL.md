# Ad spot

Ask for or infer the platform: 9:16 (Stories/TikTok), 1:1 (feed) or 16:9 (YouTube). Length 6, 15 or 30 s.
- Structure: hook in 1 s (the problem or the payoff) -> one benefit -> proof -> call to action with button-like end card. Logo visible early and at the end. Readable with the sound off.
- Make 2 hook variants as separate Compositions (HookA, HookB) when asked for testing. No claims you cannot back with the owner's own material.
Build it with the remotion-video skill (setup, render, ffprobe check, hand back the .mp4 path in the "video" field).
