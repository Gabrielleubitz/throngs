# Kinetic typography

Text drives all the motion: each word or phrase enters on the beat of its meaning (scale pop, slide, mask reveal, stagger by character with spring delays), emphasised words bigger or in the accent colour.
- Max 2 fonts (load with @remotion/google-fonts), huge size, high contrast. With narration or lyrics, get word timings first and place each word with <Sequence from>.
Build it with the remotion-video skill (setup, render, ffprobe check, hand back the .mp4 path in the "video" field).
