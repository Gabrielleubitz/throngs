# Logo intro / outro

3-6 s. Logo (supplied SVG/PNG in `public/`) revealed with a mask, scale or draw-on animation, a tagline fading in after, hold the final frame 1 s. Optional sound hit: generate or skip; never use copyrighted audio.
- Export variants: 16:9, 9:16 and 1:1 as separate Compositions on a transparent or brand-colour background (--codec=prores for alpha if asked).
Build it with the remotion-video skill (setup, render, ffprobe check, hand back the .mp4 path in the "video" field).
