# Promo video

Goal: desire in under 30 s. Structure: hook (0-3 s, the boldest claim or visual) -> 3 benefits, one scene each (3-5 s) -> proof (number, quote or logo row) -> call to action with name and URL held 3 s.
- Real product name, colours, screenshots. One idea per scene, a transition every scene, music-ready pacing (cuts on the beat, about 2 s per beat at 120 bpm).
- Big type (min 72 px at 1080p), max 7 words per card.
Build it with the remotion-video skill (setup, render, ffprobe check, hand back the .mp4 path in the "video" field).
