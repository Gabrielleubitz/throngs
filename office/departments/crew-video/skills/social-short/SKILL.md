# Social short (9:16)

Composition 1080x1920 at 30 fps, 10-30 s. Keep key content inside the safe area (inset 150 px top/bottom, 60 px sides) so app UI does not cover it.
- Hook in the first 1.5 s (a question, a surprising number, motion). Cut or change something every 2-3 s. Burned-in captions, big, high contrast, word-by-word highlight. Loop-friendly ending.
- One idea, one call to action ("follow", "link in bio") in the last 3 s.
Build it with the remotion-video skill (setup, render, ffprobe check, hand back the .mp4 path in the "video" field).
