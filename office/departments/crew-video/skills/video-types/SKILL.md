# Choosing the type of video

Library (each is its own skill, open the matching one):

| Skill | Type | Default format / length |
|---|---|---|
| promo-video | Brand or product promo, hype | 16:9, 20-30 s |
| product-demo | Walkthrough of how the product works | 16:9, 30-90 s |
| explainer-video | Teach an idea or "how it works" | 16:9, 45-90 s |
| social-short | Reel / TikTok / Short, hook-first | 9:16, 10-30 s |
| ad-spot | Paid ad with one call to action | 9:16 or 1:1, 6-15 s |
| launch-announcement | Release, feature or news announcement | 16:9, 15-30 s |
| tutorial-video | Step-by-step how-to | 16:9, 1-3 min |
| testimonial-video | Customer quote / social proof | 16:9 or 9:16, 15-30 s |
| data-story | Numbers and charts animated | 16:9, 20-45 s |
| logo-intro | Logo sting, intro or outro | 16:9, 3-6 s |
| kinetic-typography | Words as the visual (quote, lyric, statement) | any, 10-30 s |
| photo-slideshow | Photos/screens with music and captions | any, 15-60 s |
| audiogram | Podcast or audio clip with waveform and captions | 1:1 or 9:16, 15-60 s |
| event-teaser | Save-the-date / event or offer teaser | 9:16 or 16:9, 10-20 s |

## Decide the type

1. Match the request to one row by its words: "ad", "Reel/TikTok", "intro", "how to", "announce", "customer said", "numbers", "quote", "podcast", "event"...
2. If exactly one row clearly fits, say so in one line and go.
3. If two or more fit, or none, or the purpose/audience/platform is missing: DO NOT guess. Follow ask-the-owner below.

## Ask the owner (only when unsure)

Return status "needs_human" without building. In the question, use plain words and offer a short numbered list of the 3-5 most likely types, each with its one-line meaning (e.g. "1. Promo: a short hype video. 2. Demo: shows how it works..."). Also ask for anything still missing: where it will be posted (decides shape and length), and what the viewer should do at the end. The reply is one number or a few words. When the owner answers, the answer arrives in your next job: build it then.

Never ask when the type is clear. Ask once, with everything you need, not question by question.
